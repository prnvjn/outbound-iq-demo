MakeGPT, a Kochadai Technologies product incubated at IIT Madras, helps universities give students a builder's-economy mindset: real physical products from day one, Prompt → Prototype → Product, so the campus becomes a product-builder economy. LabLaunch is the internal sales and customer-success tool for selling that lab program to universities. This session walks through what the tool does today and what comes next.

What you'll see

01

University research

Vision, student headcount, alumni network, training and placement officer, address, programs, makerspaces or incubators if any, and recent news, each with its source. It works for any university, including campuses without a makerspace.

02

Custom pitch slide

A pitch slide for any university, generated live. The demo uses IIT Bombay as the example target.

03

Pitches and decks

AI-written pitches, outreach emails, and first-meeting decks tailored to each university, with fresh product ideas that fit that campus.

04

CRM

Universities, contacts, and a deal pipeline from Lead to Won, with tasks, notes, and follow-up reminders.

05

Cost control

Live API usage tracking for OpenRouter, Tavily, and Exa.

Key takeaways

  • See how a university name becomes a researched, pitch-ready opportunity.
  • See how one tool replaces scattered spreadsheets, browser tabs, and decks.
  • See how LabLaunch helps MakeGPT reach many more universities, turn each pilot into a repeatable playbook, and use alumni and placement-officer networks as warm leads.

Where the tool stands

A working version includes the CRM and deal pipeline, university research with Tavily and Exa (switchable), AI pitches, emails, and decks through OpenRouter, a customer-slide generator, and usage tracking. Next: pilot with IIT Bombay as a target university, add the first 20 target universities, and review in 30 days.
